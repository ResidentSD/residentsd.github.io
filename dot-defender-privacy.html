Dot Defender Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how the Dot Defender mobile game ("the app") handles information. We do not ask for, collect or sell your personal information. The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show those ads, as described below.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or photos.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information as described in its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy options" on the title screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings under Google > Ads (or Settings > Security and privacy > Privacy > Ads).

In-app purchase

You can buy "Remove ads" to turn off all ads; it also adds a one-time gift of in-game coins. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so that ads can be removed and the purchase can be restored on a new device.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, career milestones, trophies, coins, unlocked colors and shapes, custom icons, upgrades and whether you bought "Remove ads") is saved only on your own device, inside the app's local storage. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
